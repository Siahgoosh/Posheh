# LOCAL-SEO-QUALITY-RULES

1. No thin location pages.
2. No mass doorway city generation.
3. `unique_value` ≥ 80 chars of real local value.
4. `description` ≥ 120 chars meaningful content.
5. Coords only with `coords_verified=true`.
6. Market/price facts require `source` (+ date preferred).
7. Duplicate content ≥80% vs sibling → publish block.
8. Reviews: real/moderated only — never fabricate; no Review schema otherwise.
9. LocalBusiness schema only with verified NAP essentials.
10. AI may draft, but human approval required for market/legal/pricing claims.
11. Internal scores ≠ Google Score.
12. No Knowledge Graph presence claims.
13. Archive/redirect before deleting valuable URLs.
14. Live properties only (`active` + `show_on_website`).
15. Lead attribution must keep `city` / `source` honest.
