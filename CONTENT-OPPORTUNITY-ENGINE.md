# CONTENT-OPPORTUNITY-ENGINE

Reuses Phase 5 `SeoOpportunityEngine` (GSC/CSV):

- Quick wins (pos 4–20, high impressions)  
- CTR opportunities  
- Content decay  
- Gaps / cannibalization  

Content OS dashboard surfaces top opportunities and maps them into briefs/jobs — does **not** invent Search Console metrics.

Opportunity score = internal Demand × Business Value × Gap × Freshness — **not** a Google Score.
