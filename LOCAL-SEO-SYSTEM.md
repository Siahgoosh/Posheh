# Local SEO System

## Policy

Location pages only with real Business Value + Unique Content. No doorway spam for every city.

## Trust / NAP

- `/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`
- Contact emails from `SITE_CONTACT` (keep consistent)
- Organization JSON-LD on trust pages via `getOrganizationJsonLd()`
- No fake reviews/testimonials

## Local content guidance

Prefer real service area notes, real listings links, and honest market process content. Avoid fabricated neighborhood stats.

## Lead routing

`city` / `request_type` stored on lead for manual or future auto-routing. Auto-assign only when office mapping is configured.
