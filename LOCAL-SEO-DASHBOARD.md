# LOCAL-SEO-DASHBOARD

## Route

`/admin/seo-local` (panel: `/seo-local`)

## Widgets

- Counts: locations, published locations, topics, local articles, knowledge, live properties
- NAP / Entity consistency warnings
- Topical authority samples (internal)
- Weekly opportunities (≤10)
- Business profile NAP snapshot
- Create Location draft form

## APIs

- `GET /admin/seo/local`
- `POST /admin/seo/local/bootstrap`
- `GET /admin/seo/local/nap-scan`
- `GET /admin/seo/local/graph`
- `CRUD` locations/entities/knowledge
- `POST /admin/seo/local/opportunities/generate`
- `GET /admin/seo/local/locations/{id}/scorecard`

## Metrics honesty

Organic clicks/impressions/ROI display **UNKNOWN** unless wired to real GSC/CRM revenue later.
