## Priority 2.0 signal

Weekly actions combine:

- High impressions + low leads → CRO audit
- Low impressions + solid leads → SEO growth candidate
- Lead quality feedback from sales

Formula intent: Traffic Potential × Business Value × Conversion Potential (config-driven later). Never invent metrics.
